Draft math

That thin whistle under the door is a hole the size of a brick, the foam strip roll always runs out one window early, and the "ten-minute job" eats a Saturday. Draft math turns gap millimeters and perimeter into the open air you are really heating, doors and windows into rolls to buy and meters left over, and the whole job into cost and honest minutes. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Gap plan - gap and perimeter become open area and an equivalent round hole.
  • Strip plan - doors and windows become meters of strip, rolls and leftovers.
  • Cost plan - rolls and frames become cost and minutes of your Saturday.

Labeled norms

Labeled gap: 3 mm along a 5 m door. Labeled roll: 5 m of foam strip. Labeled install: 15 minutes per frame. Labeled comparison: the hole a gap makes, sized like a brick. These are reference values, not rules - your gaps, your frames and your rolls decide the real numbers.
